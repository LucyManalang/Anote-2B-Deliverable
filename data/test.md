# Test Document

Mira walked the forest trail every evening after work, hoping the quiet would settle her restless thoughts. One night, just as the last bit of sunlight slipped behind the hills, she noticed a faint glow deeper in the trees—a warm, flickering light like a lantern.

Curiosity tugged at her. She stepped off the path and followed the glow until she reached a small clearing. There, hanging from the branch of an old oak tree, was a lantern unlike any she had ever seen. Its glass shimmered with swirling patterns, as though tiny constellations danced inside it.

Mira touched the lantern gently, and the light grew brighter. Suddenly, memories flooded her mind: the friend she’d drifted apart from, the dream job she’d never applied for, the painting supplies collecting dust in her closet. Each memory carried the same unspoken truth—she had been waiting for life to change without ever taking a step toward it.

The lantern pulsed once, almost like a heartbeat, and the light dimmed. Mira stepped back, her breath unsteady. The forest was silent again, but something inside her had shifted. She walked home with a steadier stride, knowing that the lantern had not shown her the future—it had simply illuminated the choices she had avoided.

The next morning, she made a list of everything she wanted to begin again.
